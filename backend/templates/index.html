Prevention & Safety Guide

If you or someone you know is experiencing cyberbullying, here's how to stay safe and take action.

How to Respond
🛑
Don't Engage
Avoid responding to bullies. Reacting often encourages more harmful behavior. Take a breath and step away from the screen.
📸
Document Everything
Take screenshots of harmful messages, comments, and profiles. Include timestamps and URLs. Evidence is critical for reporting.
🚫
Block & Restrict
Use platform tools to block, mute, or restrict abusive accounts. You have the right to control your online space.
🤝
Talk to Someone
Tell a trusted adult, friend, or counselor. Cyberbullying is not your fault and you don't have to face it alone.
🔒
Secure Your Accounts
Set accounts to private, review your followers, use strong passwords, and enable two-factor authentication on all platforms.
🧘
Protect Your Mental Health
Take breaks from social media. Cyberbullying can cause real psychological harm. Seek professional support if needed.
